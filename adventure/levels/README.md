# Pirate Strike — numbered levels (engine)

Pirate Strike is planned as **100 separate levels**. Every fifth level (5, 10 … 100) is a
boss level and is part of the 100 (80 normal + 20 boss). A level is a separate playable
environment with its own start, enemies, treasure and exit. **Checkpoints are save points
inside a level, not levels.** A level unlocks only when the previous level is *completed*.

## How the player reaches Level 3
Main menu → **ADVENTURE** → **🗺 WORLD MAP** → Haven Bay. Completing its exit unlocks
Level 2; Level 2 completion unlocks Level 3. The registry still migrates the retired
Blacktide voyage's `pf-adventure-complete` flag so existing saves keep Level 2 unlocked.

**Encounters:** 35 crew — Pirate Cutthroat (fast melee pressure), Harbour Brawler (long lunge tell),
Harbour Lookout (kiting knife thrower), Powder Gunner (committed aim line, reload window) — plus the
Dockmaster. 7 checkpoints, 7 chests (3 secret: harbour ledge, crow's nest, vault), 7 breakable crates,
7 powder barrels.

**Level systems (level-01.js):**
- *Powder barrels:* strike to light (0.9 s fuse, dashed blast radius shown), strike again to kick it
  (a kicked barrel explodes on the first enemy it touches and never hurts Mari), shoot it to detonate
  at range. Blasts (120 px) hurt crew 45, Mari 20 (dash i-frames avoid it), chain other barrels, break
  crates and walls.
- *Cannon fire:* batteries only fire while Mari is inside their span; one mark per volley, aimed with a
  little lead, never during the boss fight; shells also hurt crew and light barrels.
- *Ambush:* two waves (Cutthroats, then Brawler + balcony Gunner); a death mid-ambush resets the square.
- *Breakables/crates/stalactites:* persisted in the save (`broken`, `crates`, `ambush`).
- *The Dockmaster (360 HP, unchanged slam/charge damage and hitboxes):* he **guards frontal hits with
  his anchor while idle** (30 % chip), takes full damage while recovering from his own attacks, from
  behind, from blasts and kicked kegs; super-armour means hits do not cancel his swings; a **parry**
  of the slam or charge staggers him (1.1 s). Four quick hits make him **shove** Mari away. At half
  health he **roars** (shockwave) into phase 2: **powder kegs** that land lit and can be knocked back
  into him (40 damage + 1.7 s stun) and a **signalled three-shell barrage** with a safe gap. Victory
  freezes the frame, bursts fireworks and coins, raises the portcullis and switches to the triumph cue.

**Art:** painted plates for the village, graveyard, caves and fortress, seamless 1x material faces
(cobble, hull, cave, ceiling, fortress, plaster), a roof-tile strip, three house fronts and props
(powder barrel, crate, cannon, stalactite, cracked wall, boarded door) — all from
`tools/level01_world.py` with the existing painter toolchain. They are procedural *painted* images,
not hand-illustrated professional artwork. At load the PR #96 2x terrain faces are resampled to 1x,
plates to their draw height (with aerial haze), and the Level 3 boss/enemy sheets that come with the
shared asset pack are released: decoded texture memory ≈ 39 MB (PR #96 build: ≈ 113 MB for Level 1
art alone, plus the same shared pack). Colour grade and vignette are cached layers instead of
full-screen gradients each frame.

**Audio:** music director tracks `1:haven`, `1:ambush`, `1:caves`, `1:fortress`, `1:battle`,
`1:boss`, `1:triumph`; extra effects reuse generated sounds from Levels 6, 7 and 9 (shell whistle and
boom, fuse, blast, cannon, war cry, horn).

### Level 1 combat stage (PR #96, kept)
Grounded block input opens a 160 ms parry window. A correctly faced hit in that window is negated and
deals 12 counter damage, interrupting a melee attack; later blocks retain the existing 80% damage
reduction. Powder Gunner shots use the position captured when aiming begins. Enemy AI keeps movement
inside its patrol range, checks for support before walking or charging toward an edge, and checks line
of sight against solid geometry (breakable walls and barricades included).

### Level 1 full-character sprite pipeline
The procedural part-rig has a confirmed art-quality ceiling: small separate limbs and
runtime joint rotation make fully coherent illustrated anatomy impossible. The Level 1
renderer now supports full-body animation atlases without changing enemy AI or collision
boxes. Source art must be original, transparent RGBA PNG sequences under
`<source>/<character>/<clip>/000.png`, with a shared 320×384-or-larger frame canvas and
a `<character>/character.json` containing the baseline `anchor` and optional per-frame
`points` (for example a weapon grip or gun muzzle). Authored frames are packed without
resizing by `tools/pack_level01_sprites.py`; the packer crops transparent margins, retains
offsets/anchors, records combat clip frame indices and emits a manifest. All five casts
are enabled together to avoid mixing the new visual style with the old rig. Render height
is fixed to each existing hitbox height (pirate 96, brawler 88, lookout 92, gunner 96,
Dockmaster 126 world units) and scaled by the authored silhouette height above the baseline.

Required hand-authored animation coverage: pirate (idle, walk, windup, attack, recover,
hurt, death); brawler (idle, walk, windup, lunge, recover, hurt, death); lookout (idle,
walk, aim, throw, recover, hurt, death); gunner (idle, walk, aim, fire, reload, hurt,
death); Dockmaster (idle, walk, slamWind, slam, recover, hurt, death). Locomotion clips
loop; combat/reaction clips play through once. Atlas frame metadata supports named
weapon/projectile points while current gameplay projectile spawn positions remain
unchanged. The full-frame renderer is Level 1-only; other levels continue using the
shared cutout rig.

**Art status:** no suitable original enemy full-body animation sheets exist in the
repository for the pirate, brawler, lookout, gunner, or Dockmaster. Existing illustrated
hero sheets are not substituted or recolored as enemies. `manifest.json` therefore remains
`art_pending`, and the game deliberately continues using the existing fallback art until
all five approved original character atlases are authored. Existing Mari-versus-enemy
screenshots show that fallback, not the planned redesigned art; replacement screenshots
must wait for the real source frames.

| Area | Content |

| D · Underground Treasure Chamber | Descending caves, underground waterfall, two skeletons, archer, dart trap (low/high darts with telegraphs). **Puzzle 2**: break the cracked wall (3 hits) → secret mechanism disables the darts and powers the vault lift → hidden treasure vault (60 gold). | E · Guardian's Gate | Checkpoint, arena locks behind you, **Ancient Guardian** miniboss, great gate opens → exit completes the level and unlocks Level 4. |

### Enemies
* **Skeleton Warrior** — patrols, chases, raises its sword (0.48 s red telegraph) then slashes (16).
* **Shield Guardian** — blocks every frontal hit ("BLOCKED"). Charges with a glowing shield bash (20)
  after a 0.62 s wind-up, then is **stunned for 1.15 s** (vulnerable). Heavy 3rd combo hit from behind also works.
* **Cursed Archer** — 0.85 s visible draw with a dashed aim line, fires a slow glowing arrow (15).
  Arrows can be jumped, blocked, dashed through or cut out of the air.
* **Ancient Guardian** (miniboss, 680 HP, not the Level 5 main boss) — entrance roar/music; patterns:
  cleave with red danger zone → sword gets stuck (open window); leap with landing ring → slam + two
  ground shockwaves (jump them); at 50 % it enrages (orange runes, knock-back roar) and adds a
  3-crystal arcing volley. Health bar with rage marker, hit flashes, debris, rubble death, coin drop.

### Combat (same rules as Blacktide / Combat V2)
Combo A-B-C (23/23/34) by tapping or holding ATTACK, BLOCK (80 % frontal reduction), DASH
(0.17 s, i-frames, 0.85 s cooldown), THROW (3 swords, reload 1/s on the floor), double jump,
coyote time and jump buffering. Levers, walls and the mechanism are struck with the sword
(or a thrown sword) — no keyboard-only interaction.

### Save and progression
* `pf-level-03-v1`: checkpoint, HP, lives, gold, collected coins, defeated enemies, opened chests and
  puzzle state. Saved on checkpoints, chests, puzzles, pause, exit and every 5 s.
* Death → respawn at the last checkpoint. Game over → retry keeps checkpoint, gold and kills.
  RESTART LEVEL starts from the entrance with fresh state. Corrupt saves fall back to a clean start.
* Completion → summary (gold, time, enemies, treasure, secrets) → `PirateLevels.complete(3)` → Level 4
  unlocked; the per-level save is cleared so a replay starts at the entrance. Gold is added to
  `pf-adventure-gold`.

## Art and audio
Original HD pixel art at 2× (64×64 actor frames, 128×128 miniboss), generated by
`tools/characters.py`/`environment.py`: hero captain (24 frames), skeleton (12), shield guardian (12),
archer (8), Ancient Guardian (14 + enraged palette), 3 parallax layers, 16 tiles, 48 props.
Runtime adds animated torches, crystals, waterfalls, light shafts, fog, dust motes, dynamic lighting
(quarter-resolution light map), sword trails, sparks, hit-stop, damage numbers, debris, splash,
coin flights, chest rays, gate animations and victory particles. Effects respect reduced motion
and the existing *Hit effects* setting; particle lists are capped.

Audio (`tools/audio.py`, Ogg Vorbis, ~2.6 MB): ruins music (46 s loop), Guardian battle music
(32 s loop), victory sting, wind/insect, cave-drip and waterfall ambience loops, and 57 effects
(stone footsteps, three swing variants, impacts, enemy attacks/deaths, gold, chest, lever, rune,
gate, wall break, mechanism, darts, spikes, crumble, miniboss intro/roar/slam/death…). Separate
music/ambience/effects buses under a compressor; music sits below effects; 1.2–1.6 s cross-fades;
the existing sound setting mutes everything; AudioContext is resumed only from a user gesture.

* **Ending**: the Captain falls, strike the lamp winch three times → the beacon ignites (plate cross-fades
